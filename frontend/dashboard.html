HERE TO HELP

Help & Support

Quick answers to help you make the most of a safe, thoughtful exchange.

How do I start an exchange?

Publish a course you own, browse another member’s listing, choose an offer and duration, then send a request. The course owner must approve it.

⇄
What gets exchanged?

Only course details. Never share course videos, presentations, downloads, passwords, or account access.

◇
Is a payment processed here?

No. ₹49 per user is informational only; SkillSwap does not collect or process payments.

₹
How do I arrange a session?

Once a request is accepted, open Exchange Requests and use Schedule to add a meeting to both members’ calendars.
